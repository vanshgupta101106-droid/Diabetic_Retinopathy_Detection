ICDR Grading System

The International Clinical Diabetic Retinopathy (ICDR) system classifies diabetic retinopathy into 5 severity stages based on the presence and extent of microvascular lesions.

0
No Apparent DR
No lesions detected
1
Mild NPDR
Microaneurysms only
2
Moderate NPDR
Dot hemorrhages, hard exudates
3
Severe NPDR
Venous beading, IRMA, 20+ hemorrhages/quadrant
4
Proliferative DR
Neovascularization — urgent intervention needed

Key Pathological Lesions

🔴
Dot Hemorrhages
Small round intraretinal bleeds from microaneurysm rupture.
🟡
Hard Exudates
Lipid deposits from leaky capillaries; bright yellow/white spots.
🟠
Cotton-Wool Spots
Nerve fiber layer infarcts appearing as fluffy white patches.
🩸
Neovascularization
New fragile blood vessels; marker of proliferative DR.
🔵
Microaneurysms
Earliest DR sign: small outpouchings of capillary walls.
〰️
Venous Beading
String-of-beads appearance; marker of severe NPDR.
